# Codeply Wisp film v2: voiceover script (Eleven v4)

The film is 85 seconds long, on the same 118 BPM track as v1. There are 12 lines, each timed to a scene.

## How to make it in ElevenLabs

1. Open **Text to Speech** and pick the model **Eleven v4**. Use plain v4, not v4 Turbo; Turbo is the real-time variant.
2. Pick the voice (see below).
3. Paste the **whole script block** below in one go. v4 performs best when it reads a full passage instead of separate sentences. The `[silence]` tags leave gaps between lines, so I can cut them apart and place each line on its beat.
4. **Settings:**
   - **Stability:** around the middle (Natural). Lower it a little if the read feels flat; raise it if it gets theatrical.
   - **Similarity:** about 75%.
   - **Style and Speed:** v4 has no sliders for these. Pace comes from the tags and punctuation, which are already in the script.
5. Generate two or three takes and pick the calmest, most natural one. If one line misses, keep the words and change only its tag, as ElevenLabs suggests.
6. Download it as MP3 (or WAV) and send it to me. One file is fine. I'll cut it into lines and line each one up with its scene.

**The free plan:** it includes v4 and about 10 minutes of audio a month, which is plenty for this script. But free-plan audio isn't licensed for commercial use and has to credit ElevenLabs. Use it for testing; for the version you publish, generate the final take on Starter ($5 a month, which adds commercial rights).

## The voice

**What we want:** a warm, calm, quietly confident woman in her late 20s to 30s, with a neutral American accent. She should sound like a capable friend who already has things handled, not a hyped announcer. Wisp is ambient: it pays attention so you don't have to, and the voice should feel the same way. Unhurried, smiling a little.

**Voices to try** (from the ElevenLabs library; test the first and last lines with each):

1. **Sarah**. Confident, warm and reassuring. My first pick.
2. **Matilda**. Friendly, with an alto pitch. Calmer and a little older.
3. **Jessica**. Bright and playful. Try her if Sarah feels too serious.

If you'd rather use a male voice, try **Brian** (deep narration) or **Liam** (younger, energetic).

**Pronunciation:**
- **Codeply:** "CODE-plee". If v4 says it wrong, write `Code-plee` in the text.
- **Alt and C:** two keys, "Alt" then "C".

## The script (paste all of this)

```
[warm, unhurried, a quiet smile] Meet Wisp. It lives inside Codeply... and it pays attention, so you don't have to.
[silence]
[relaxed, matter-of-fact] An important email lands while you're busy? Wisp spots it, and drafts the reply. [reassuring] It never sends a thing without you.
[silence]
[light, friendly] Ask once, and every morning, it tells you what matters.
[silence]
[curious, conversational] Stuck on something? Hold Alt and C... and just ask. Wisp sees your screen, points to the answer, and walks you through it.
[silence]
[playful, a little faster] Or say "just do it"... and it takes over, with its own cursor.
[silence]
[confident, warm] It can show you. Explain it. Or simply do it.
[silence]
[focused, steady] Give it real work, like a bug in your checkout. Wisp brings in the right specialists, and runs every test.
[silence]
[calm, reassuring] Close your laptop. It keeps going in the cloud... and calls you when it's ready.
[silence]
[warm, proud] Behind it, a whole team of specialists, each with its own job.
[silence]
[soft, rhythmic] Your mail. Your code. Your day.
[silence]
[easygoing] It works with the apps and models you already use.
[silence]
[warm, slow, landing it] Codeply Wisp. [short pause] Your day, handled. [softer] Before you ask.
```
